# Smart Supply Chain Management Platform with AI

## Detailed Project Description

# 1. Introduction

The Smart Supply Chain Management Platform is an enterprise-grade web
application that manages the complete lifecycle of products from
procurement to customer delivery. It connects suppliers, procurement
teams, warehouses, logistics teams, administrators, and business
managers through a centralized platform.

Unlike conventional inventory management systems, this project
integrates Artificial Intelligence, microservices, cloud technologies,
and modern software engineering practices to improve operational
efficiency and business decision-making.

The project is inspired by enterprise supply chain systems used by
Amazon, Flipkart, Walmart, DHL, FedEx, and manufacturing organizations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Vision

To build a scalable, secure, intelligent, and cloud-ready platform that
automates supply chain operations while using AI to forecast demand,
optimize inventory, recommend suppliers, and provide business insights.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Problem Statement

Organizations often face: - Overstocking and stock shortages - Manual
inventory tracking - Delayed supplier deliveries - Poor warehouse
utilization - Lack of demand forecasting - Inefficient logistics
planning - Fragmented software systems - Limited business visibility

This platform addresses these challenges through automation, analytics,
and AI.

# 7. Artificial Intelligence Features

## AI Demand Forecasting

Predicts future demand using historical sales and seasonal trends.

## AI Inventory Optimization

Identifies dead stock, slow-moving inventory, overstock, and recommends
actions.

## AI Supplier Recommendation

Ranks suppliers using cost, quality, lead time, and reliability.

## AI Procurement Planner

Generates purchase recommendations and estimated budgets.

## AI Logistics Optimization

Suggests optimized delivery routes and shipment plans.

## AI Business Assistant

Natural-language assistant answering business questions using company
data (RAG).

## AI Risk Detection

Detects unusual inventory changes, delayed suppliers, and abnormal
purchasing patterns.
